PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
PassReady Prep is an exam-preparation app for counselors preparing for the NCMHCE (National Clinical Mental Health Counseling Examination), the case-based licensure exam required for LPC/LMHC licensure in most US states. Candidates often struggle because they have not practiced the exam's case format and weighted scoring. The app provides NCMHCE-style clinical case simulations with weighted scoring and explanations, timed full-length mock exams, a readiness score by exam domain, flashcards, decision trees and a DSM-5-TR quick reference. Audience: adults (18+) preparing for professional counseling licensure. It is study material only; it does not provide clinical care or clinical advice.

HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
Demo account with full paid access: demo@passreadyprep.com (password in the Sign-In Information above).
1. Launch the app, tap "Start studying" to open the study dashboard.
2. Tap "Sign in" (top right) and sign in with the demo account.
3. Case simulations: choose a case, answer each question; points and feedback appear after each answer, with an AI debrief at the end.
4. Timed mock exam: "Timed Mock Exam" on the dashboard, then "Begin Exam".
5. Other tools on the dashboard: Flashcards, Decision Trees, DSM-5-TR Reference, Knowledge Drill, Intake Interview Simulator (microphone optional; typing also works).
6. Account creation: "Create account" on the sign-in page. New accounts get a 3-day free trial of full access.
7. Account deletion: dashboard → "Account" button (top right) → "Delete my account…" → enter password → tick the confirmation → "Delete my account" → confirmation screen. Deletion is immediate and permanent and does not require contacting us. The same link is at the bottom of the dashboard ("Delete account").
8. Purchases (In-App Purchase): plans are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Landing page → Pricing → choose a plan (or Dashboard → a locked tool → "See plans"), then Buy. No account or registration is needed to buy or to restore: a signed-out purchase is kept for this device, and the purchase screen and Account page explain that creating a free account is optional and lets the plan be used on the person's other devices. "Restore purchases" is on the purchase screen and on the Account page. Subscriptions can be managed from Account → "Manage subscription" (the App Store sheet).
No sample files are needed.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Anthropic (Claude API): generates AI feedback and case debriefs from the user's answers.
- ElevenLabs: text-to-speech voice for intake-simulator replies.
- iOS speech recognition (WebKit): converts spoken answers to text in the intake simulator.
- Stripe: payments for plans bought on our website (passreadyprep.com). In the app, purchases use Apple In-App Purchase only; the app does not link to web checkout.
- Brevo: account emails (password reset, receipts).
- MongoDB Atlas: database for accounts and study progress.
- Render: hosting for our server at passreadyprep.com.
- Google Translate widget: optional on-page translation when the user picks a language.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app is distributed on the United States storefront only and works the same for all users. In the app, plans are bought through Apple In-App Purchase. People who bought a plan on passreadyprep.com can sign in to use it.

TRACKING
The app does not track users: no advertising SDK, no data brokers, no linking with third-party data. App Privacy answers are set accordingly (no data type is used for tracking), so no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown.
