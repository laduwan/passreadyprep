About the study guide

The guide teaches one repeatable method for every NCE question: read the last line first, name the owner of each option, predict, then eliminate by owner. NCE distractors are rarely nonsense — they are real counseling terms that belong to a different theory, theorist, test, ethical standard, or diagnosis. Once you can name the owner, the right answer is the only one left standing.
