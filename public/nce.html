NCE practice questions: what the exam covers

The National Counselor Examination (NCE) is NBCC’s 200-question, multiple-choice exam used for the National Certified Counselor credential and, in many states, for counselor licensure. You have 3 hours 45 minutes. NBCC scores 160 of the 200 items; the rest are unscored pretest questions you cannot identify, so treat every question as if it counts.

The six NCE content domains

NBCC organizes the NCE around six domains, and the exam weights them unequally. PassReady Prep’s question bank and timed mocks are weighted the same way, and your progress is tracked by domain so you can see which one is costing you points:

  • Professional Counseling Orientation and Ethical Practice
  • Social and Cultural Diversity
  • Human Growth and Development
  • Career Development
  • Counseling and Helping Relationships
  • Group Counseling and Group Work, plus Assessment and Testing, and Research and Program Evaluation

How to study for the NCE

The NCE rewards breadth. Most candidates lose points on the smaller domains (career theory, research design, assessment statistics) because they study what they already know. Start with a timed set to get a baseline by domain, then spend most of your time on your two weakest domains. Use the theories and pioneers reference for the counseling-theory items, which recur heavily, and the Theory Clue and Code Match games for recall on the days you cannot sit down to a full set. Review every missed question’s rationale; the rationale teaches the concept the next version of that question will test.

Full-length timed mock exams

The mock exam follows the real structure: 200 questions, a 225-minute clock, and an optional break at the halfway point. You get no feedback until you submit, then a full domain breakdown and a review of every missed item. Take your first mock early for a baseline, then one per week in the final month.
