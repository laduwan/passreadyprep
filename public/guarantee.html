1

Common questions

How do I claim my free CE course after passing?
Email your unofficial NCMHCE score report to us within 14 days. We'll send you a coupon code for your choice of TMH 601 or Mandated Reporter. Then sign up for a free account at CounselorReady.com, apply your code, and enable CE deadline reminders in your dashboard. We'll email you at 60, 30, and 7 days so you complete the course before your 90-day window closes.
Can I get a refund instead of extended access if I fail?
The guarantee covers extended access, not a refund. We want to keep helping you pass — that's the point.
How long does my access last if I don't pass?
Once your non-passing result is verified, your access continues so you can prepare for your retake. No repayment is required.
Why do you verify a non-passing result?
To keep each guarantee tied to one candidate and prevent account sharing. The name on your score report must match your account, and we may ask for your official result.
Is there a limit on how many times I can use the guarantee?
Yes. Each Pass Guarantee is single use and can be claimed once. To cover a later attempt after it has been claimed, purchase a new Pass Guarantee.
What counts as a "completed case"?
A case where you answered all questions and saw the results screen. Starting and abandoning a case doesn't count.
Does the guarantee cover the NCE?
Yes, through the separate NCE Pass Guarantee. See Taking the NCE? above. Each guarantee covers only its own exam.
What is CounselorReady?
CounselorReady is our board-approved continuing education platform for licensed counselors. Once you pass your exam and get licensed, you'll need CE credits — your free course gets you started.
