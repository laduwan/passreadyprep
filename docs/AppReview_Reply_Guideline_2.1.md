# App Review reply — Guideline 2.1 Information Needed (v1.0)

## Part A — paste into App Store Connect (App Review → reply to the message)

Thank you for reviewing PassReady Prep. Here is the requested information. The same details are now in the App Review Information notes.

**1. Screen recording**
Attached. Recorded on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS. It begins at app launch and shows: creating an account, signing in, a clinical case simulation with scoring and the AI debrief, the timed mock exam, flashcards, choosing a plan (which opens our website in Safari, per the US storefront external-purchase link), and deleting the account (dashboard → Delete account). The app has no user-generated content shared with other users, so content reporting and blocking do not apply.

**2. Purpose and target audience**
PassReady Prep is an exam-preparation app for counselors preparing for the NCMHCE (National Clinical Mental Health Counseling Examination), the case-based licensure exam required for LPC/LMHC licensure in most US states. Candidates often struggle because they have not practiced the exam's case format and weighted scoring. The app provides NCMHCE-style clinical case simulations with weighted scoring and explanations, timed full-length mock exams, a readiness score by exam domain, flashcards, decision trees and a DSM-5-TR quick reference. Audience: adults (18+) preparing for professional counseling licensure. It is study material only; it does not provide clinical care or clinical advice.

**3. Setup and access**
Demo account with full paid access: demo@passreadyprep.com (password in the Sign-In Information above).
1. Launch the app, tap "Start studying" to open the study dashboard.
2. Tap "Sign in" (top right) and sign in with the demo account.
3. Case simulations: choose a case, answer each question; points and feedback appear after each answer, with an AI debrief at the end.
4. Timed mock exam: "Timed Mock Exam" on the dashboard, then "Begin Exam".
5. Other tools on the dashboard: Flashcards, Decision Trees, DSM-5-TR Reference, Knowledge Drill, Intake Interview Simulator (microphone optional; typing also works).
6. Account creation: "Create account" on the sign-in page. New accounts get a 3-day free trial of full access.
7. Account deletion: dashboard, scroll to the bottom, "Delete account", enter password, confirm. Monthly subscribers can cancel from "Manage subscription" in the same place.
No sample files are needed.

**4. External services**
- Anthropic (Claude API): generates AI feedback and case debriefs from the user's answers.
- ElevenLabs: text-to-speech voice for intake-simulator replies.
- iOS speech recognition (WebKit): converts spoken answers to text in the intake simulator.
- Stripe: subscription payments, processed on our website only (no payments inside the app).
- Brevo: account emails (password reset, receipts).
- MongoDB Atlas: database for accounts and study progress.
- Render: hosting for our server at passreadyprep.com.
- Google Translate widget: optional on-page translation when the user picks a language.

**5. Regional differences**
The app is distributed on the United States storefront only and works the same for all users. Subscriptions and passes are purchased on our website through the external purchase link permitted for US storefront apps; no in-app purchase is offered. Users who already purchased on the web can sign in to access their plan.

**6. Regulated industry / third-party material**
PassReady Prep is operated by GA Integrated Therapeutic Perspectives LLC, an NBCC Approved Continuing Education Provider (ACEP No. 7760). The app is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NBCC; "NCMHCE" is used descriptively to name the exam. All case simulations, questions, flashcards and explanations are original content written by our team. The DSM-5-TR reference uses our own summaries and public ICD-10-CM codes and does not reproduce copyrighted DSM text. The app does not provide clinical services and is not a medical device.
