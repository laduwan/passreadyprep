### 2. nce-s-eth-074 · Professional Counseling Orientation and Ethical Practice · record keeping and documentation · medium · In review

**B. [ ]** Edit the dose in the original note quietly so the record reads correctly

> The monkeys clung to the cloth mother and ran to her when frightened, even though the wire mother fed them.

**B. [ ]** Attachment forms to whichever surrogate supplies food
> This drive-reduction view is what Harlow's results contradicted.

**C. [ ]** Infants form no attachment to either surrogate mother
> The monkeys formed a clear attachment to the cloth surrogate.

**D. [ ]** Imprinting occurs only in a narrow window after birth

A counselor tells a 45-year-old Mexican American client, "It must help to have your big family around you right now." The client replies, "Actually, my family is part of the stress. My friends from work are who I lean on." Which counselor response BEST reflects cultural humility?

> This holds on to the counselor's assumption about family support and quietly dismisses the client's correction.

### 190. nce-s-cor-054 · Counseling and Helping Relationships · nonjudgmental stance · easy · In review

### 195. nce-s-cor-059 · Counseling and Helping Relationships · empathy and its levels · hard · In review
